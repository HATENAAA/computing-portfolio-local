Remote Sensing Image Super-Resolution Reconstruction Based on Deep Learning

I reproduced MCArb on MCNet, SSPSR and ERCSR, and developed a Python workflow for per-image evaluation on Pavia Centre and Chikusei after MATLAB preprocessing.

Across backbones

MCArb-SSPSR improved all six metrics relative to SSPSR-b on both datasets at ×4 and ×8. PSNR gains ranged from 0.21 to 0.36 dB. The thesis averages its metrics per image. The separate workbench can also give scenes equal weight.

Process and results

MATLAB prepares paired low- and high-resolution cubes. Training covers three baselines and their MCArb variants. The Python evaluation compares reconstructed images with references across six metrics.
