The thesis reproduces the MCArb method by Zhang et al. (IEEE TGRS, 2025, DOI 10.1109/TGRS.2025.3544253). The archived source retains its AGPL licence and upstream copyright notices. Project credits distinguish original research, implementation, team contributions and external datasets.

毕业论文复现MCArb框架：Zhang et al., Meta-Collaborative Learning for Arbitrarily Scaled Hyperspectral Image Super-Resolution, IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing, 2025. DOI: 10.1109/TGRS.2025.3544253。个人工作包括框架复现、对比与消融实验、Python评价流程和重建可视化。

方法源码的AGPL许可证及原版权声明、WebXR场景组织的参考归属说明、ONNX分类器与测试视频的数据许可随各项目源码保存。Vue、Three.js、Spring Boot、OpenCV及其他依赖适用各自许可证。
